India is known for its diverse and flavorful cuisine, which varies from region to region.
From spicy curries to sweet desserts, Indian food offers a wide range of tastes and textures.
Some of the most famous dishes include Butter Chicken, Biryani, Dosa, Poori, and Vada.
Each dish has its own unique preparation method and ingredients, making Indian cuisine a delightful experience for food lovers.

Butter Chicken

Butter Chicken is a popular North Indian dish made with marinated chicken cooked in a rich tomato-based sauce with butter and cream.
It is often served with naan or rice.


Biryani

Biryani is a popular rice dish from the Indian subcontinent, made with basmati rice, meat (or vegetables),
and a blend of spices. It is often served with raita or salad.

Dosa

Dosa is a popular South Indian dish made from a fermented batter of rice and lentils.
It is often served with chutney or sambar.
