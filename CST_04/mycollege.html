WELCOME TO CHITKARA UNIVERSITY

INTRODUCTION


Chitkara University is a well-known private university located in Punjab, India.
The Punjab campus was established by the Chitkara Educational Trust, and Chitkara University
was established in 2010 under the Chitkara University Act. It is recognised under the relevant
provisions of the UGC Act.The university offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate
and doctoral programmes in fields such as Engineering, Computer Applications, AI etc.
The university also provides modern infrastructure, laboratories, extracurricular activities,
industry collaborations and opportunities for professional development. Its main aim
is to prepare students with the knowledge, skills and practical experience required for their
future careers. Chitkara University has become a popular choice among students in North
India because of its student-centric and industry-focused approach to education.
